# North star

Beez is a voice infrastructure that allows anyone to shop, sell, and manage a business simply by speaking. It turns everyday voice into orders, stores, payments, and business insights, so customers can buy without typing and merchants can run their entire shop without paperwork. Built for Africa, it speaks English, Swahili, French, Haitian Creole and more, and works even on low data and through WhatsApp. From street vendors to growing shops, Beez makes commerce as natural as having a conversation.

## Metric

Orders confirmed with merchants each week
